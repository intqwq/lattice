# Mathematics content: provenance and coverage

Authored 28 September 2026. The 25 bilingual lessons, 120 sections and 50 exercises in math.mjs are original explanations, examples and questions. Primary educational sources below were consulted to verify definitions, theorem conditions, terminology and scope; no textbook diagrams, passages, exercises or solutions were copied. Source links require a network connection, but the authored lesson content itself is bundled for offline reading.

2026 年 9 月 28 日编写。math.mjs 中的 25 节双语课、120 个教学段落与 50 道练习均为原创讲解、例子与题目。下列一手教育资料用于核对定义、定理条件、术语与范围；没有复制教材图片、段落、练习或解答。资料链接需要联网，但课程正文随应用离线提供。

## Actual coverage

The starter path runs from counting and place value through signed arithmetic, rational numbers, algebra, functions, polynomial-first calculus, vectors and introductory linear algebra. A separate probability introduction supports genetics and computing. Every prerequisite refers to an existing lesson; the graph was checked for missing references and cycles.

This is not diploma equivalence, a complete mathematics curriculum, or a complete treatment of any course containing these modules. Trigonometry, systematic Euclidean geometry, logarithms, number theory, combinatorics, integration techniques, series, multivariable calculus and abstract algebra still need their own detailed lessons. Most modules contain only an introductory lesson.

起步路线覆盖计数与位值、有符号运算、有理数、代数、函数、以多项式为先的微积分、向量及初步线性代数。概率入门支持遗传学与计算机科学。先修引用均指向已存在的课，且已检查缺失引用与环。

本内容不等同于高中学历水平、完整数学课程或任何所属课程的完整教学。三角学、系统欧氏几何、对数、数论、组合学、积分技巧、级数、多元微积分与抽象代数等仍需要专门详细课程。大多数模块当前只有入门课。

## Review boundary

Syntax, bilingual field presence, course/module mapping, prerequisite references, graph acyclicity and choice-answer membership were checked locally. Calculations and proofs were self-reviewed. Independent subject-expert review, learner trials and assessment calibration have not been performed. Estimated minutes are session planning aids, not promises of mastery.

已进行语法、双语字段、课程模块映射、先修引用、无环性及选择题答案有效性检查；计算与证明经过自查。尚未进行独立学科专家审校、学习者试用或评估标定。预计分钟数仅用于安排学习，不保证达到掌握。
