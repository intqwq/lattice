# Science foundation pack — editorial notes

Verified reference access: 28 September 2026. The lesson file contains 18 authored bilingual lessons: six physics/shared measurement, six chemistry, and six biology. It is a beginning content pack, not a claim of complete school, university or Olympiad coverage.

Each lesson has five explanation blocks, original worked scenarios, learning objectives, explicit prerequisite lesson IDs, two or three deterministic exercises, hints and complete authored explanations in both languages. Numeric prompts specify the required unit. A successful answer checks the stated item; it does not certify broad mastery or laboratory competence.

## Source use

The text, translation, examples and exercises in `science.mjs` were written for Lattice. No textbook pages, source illustrations, source problem sets, figures or videos are bundled. Per-lesson `sources` records link the primary educational references used to check terminology, conceptual scope and factual statements. References are optional online reading; the authored lessons are available offline.

- [BIPM SI base units](https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units/si-base-units) and [defining constants](https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units/si-defining-constants) anchor unit names and the exact Avogadro constant. Some introductory textbooks retain historical wording about unit definitions; this pack uses the current SI constant-based mole definition.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare Classical Mechanics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-01sc-classical-mechanics-fall-2016/) provides a university mechanics reference. It is not presented as a prerequisite for the algebra-based introductory lessons.
- OpenStax College Physics 2e and University Physics Volume 1 references cover units, motion, forces, energy and work. Chemistry 2e references cover classification, particles, mole calculations, conservation, solutions and bonding. Biology 2e references cover scientific reasoning, cellular organization, membranes, metabolism, DNA and Mendelian inheritance. Exact section URLs are attached to the corresponding lessons.

Linking a reference is not a grant to redistribute its assets. The current OpenStax pages display CC BY-NC-SA terms and additional reuse conditions; this pack therefore does not assume a generic unrestricted textbook-content licence. Future inclusion of any third-party text, images or exercises requires an asset-specific provenance and licence decision.

## Explicit model boundaries

- Mechanics uses ordinary nonrelativistic motion, chosen reference directions and stated constant-force or smooth-function assumptions. The derivative and integral lessons require specific mathematics lessons; introductory motion does not require calculus.
- Measurement uncertainty examples are estimates, not guaranteed bounds for every error. Precise repetition does not prove accuracy.
- Chemistry separates element identity, isotope, ion, molecule and formula unit. The mole is exactly defined while teaching molar masses are explicitly rounded. Balanced equations express conservation and do not establish reaction feasibility or a safe laboratory procedure.
- Osmosis examples specify equal starting pressure, membrane selectivity and nonpenetrating solute. Concentration alone is not treated as enough to establish biological tonicity in every system.
- Enzyme explanations distinguish activation barriers from thermodynamic endpoint differences. ATP is explained through the whole coupled reaction, not the misleading claim that bond breaking by itself releases energy.
- DNA complementary-sequence exercises explicitly state aligned positions to avoid silent strand-orientation ambiguity.
- Genetics uses an explicit diploid, equal-segregation model and independent gamete/offspring assumptions. Dominance, probability and expected counts are not described as inevitability or human-trait diagnosis.
- Pictures and interactions are teaching models. They do not establish real laboratory or instrumentation competence.

## Follow-on content still required

The pack needs expansion into complete concept inventories, multiple independent and delayed retrieval forms, experimental reasoning, and practical assessment before a full course can claim comprehensive coverage. In particular, these introductory lessons do not replace complete treatment of electromagnetism, waves, thermodynamics, chemical equilibrium, organic chemistry, organismal physiology, ecology or advanced genetics. The existing curriculum routes retain those as planned work.
