# Course catalogue and learning routes

Design proposal · 28 September 2026 · All courses and modules below are **planned**, not published lessons.

The catalogue contains **90 courses and 540 named modules**. Course titles are bilingual; full module and lesson translations will be reviewed during authoring. H = school foundations; U = undergraduate; O = Olympiad; E = advanced elective. These are independent tags, not a ladder that makes university completion mandatory before Olympiad study.

**How to read a route:** follow its six modules in the displayed order, with placement-based skips and optional deeper branches. Readiness references identify knowledge used somewhere in the full course. They are not whole-course entry locks. Exact lesson-level prerequisites must be authored before a course can be published. Exit tasks are proposed assessment targets, not evidence of learner mastery.

## Coverage and authoring boundaries

The catalogue covers the requested subjects and a broad undergraduate core. It does not claim to include every specialized degree elective or every competition syllabus. Examples of later packs include measure-theoretic probability, PDEs, algebraic number theory, advanced materials, specialized organismal biology, robotics, and deeper programming-language theory. Extensions must connect to the existing graph rather than duplicate foundational lessons.

Olympiad studios reference topic families, not a guaranteed competition result or national selection path. Current competition alignment is separately versioned. Biology practicals and chemistry/physics laboratory technique require supervised physical work; software can teach reasoning and analyze evidence but cannot certify manual proficiency.

Rebuild and validate the catalogue with `node blueprint/build-blueprint.mjs` from the project root. See `validation.json` for structural checks and their limits.
